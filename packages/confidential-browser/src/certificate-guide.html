How to check this browser's certificate
  1. Export this tab's leaf certificate. Use the browser's certificate viewer, not the page's download. Choose the site's certificate, rather than its issuer or a certificate chain, and export DER or PEM. In Chrome or Edge, open DevTools → Security → View certificate. In Firefox, open the site's connection details → More information → View Certificate. If an embedded browser cannot show its certificate, open the portal in a browser that can and check that browser's connection.
  2. Read its fingerprint and validity. For a DER export: 
    openssl x509 -inform DER -in browser-leaf.der \
  -noout -fingerprint -sha256 -dates

    For PEM, omit -inform DER and use your PEM filename. Add -text to inspect X.509 details and extensions; OpenSSL does not validate the embedded AMD evidence. The certificate fingerprint hashes the entire DER certificate. The TLS public-key fingerprint hashes its DER SubjectPublicKeyInfo (SPKI); these are different values. A renewed certificate may keep the same public key. Neither fingerprint nor validity alone verifies attestation.

  3. Verify its attestation independently. Use a verifier obtained from a source you trust and an approved policy from an independently trusted release or review. With a reviewed TNG build: 
    tng tools rats-tls verify --cert browser-leaf.der \
  --verify "$(cat approved-verify.json)"

    The same command accepts PEM. --verify takes JSON text, not a filename. Use an approved built-in CoCo configuration. Its verifier checks AMD SNP evidence and its binding to the certificate key. Your independently approved policy must require the approved VM launch measurement, native workload-policy digest (HOST_DATA / init_data), minimum TCB, and disabled debug and migration. Check chip identity when your policy requires it. This needs no external Trustee.

    Success exits with code 0 and prints verified OK followed by an attestation-result JWT. Stop on an error or nonzero exit. Decoding a JWT to read its claims does not verify its signature or policy.

The page's checker and /certificate.der download are conveniences. Verify the browser-exported certificate; if you use a reported download instead, first compare its complete certificate fingerprint with this tab's actual leaf. Matching a public-key fingerprint alone is not a full attestation check. Expected policy values supplied only by this page are not an independent trust source. A fresh nonce cannot authenticate the JavaScript that displays its own result. Enter access tokens, passwords and registry credentials only after your independent check succeeds.
